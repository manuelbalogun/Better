## Full description (4,000 max)
Take your meds on time. Better reminds you around the moments of your day, not just a clock: wake up, breakfast, lunch, dinner and bedtime.

HOW IT WORKS
• Set your day once. Better ties each medicine to a moment, like breakfast or bedtime.
• Take everything for a moment in one tap, or snooze it.
• Running late? Move that moment's reminder for today only.
• See your week, month and six months at a glance.
• Get a heads-up before your pills run out.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Free. No account. No ads.
• Everything stays on your phone. Better has no internet permission and cannot send your data anywhere.
• Back up to a file you keep, and restore any time.

BUILT FOR RELIABLE REMINDERS
• Exact alarms, restored after a restart.
• A reliability check that shows what to turn on for your phone, including Tecno, Infinix, Xiaomi and Samsung.
• Light and dark themes.

Better is a reminder tool, not medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
