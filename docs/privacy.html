Better – Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Better is a free medication reminder app for Android. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and we collect nothing.

What Better stores

Better stores the following on your phone, inside the app's private storage: your first name (optional), the medicines you add, your daily times, the reminders you take, skip or snooze, and your settings. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Better has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no internet permission. The developer cannot see your data.

Permissions Better asks for

  • Notifications, to remind you to take your medicine.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms), so reminders arrive on the minute.
  • Run at start-up, so reminders are restored after your phone restarts.
  • Vibrate, if you keep vibration turned on for reminders.

Backups

If you tap “Export backup”, Better creates one file and opens Android's share sheet so you can choose where to keep it, for example in your files or in an app you trust. Better does not upload it. If you share the file, the place you choose is responsible for it. Restoring a backup reads a file you pick on your phone.

Deleting your data

Open Settings, then “Delete all my data” in Better, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything Better stored on your phone. Backup files you exported are separate files and stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Better is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Better is a reminder tool. It does not give medical advice, check doses or interactions, or replace advice from your doctor or pharmacist. Follow the instructions on your prescription.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
